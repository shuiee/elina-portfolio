<Section id="question" label="The question">

<p class="lede">{frontmatter.question}</p>

TooGoodToGo works on a straightforward trade: the shop has food it cannot sell, and you
get it cheaply on the understanding that you do not get to choose what it is. The
surprise is the point, and the discount is what makes the surprise acceptable.

Korea's dining culture has moved toward health-conscious eating, where people are
often choosing a meal on what is in it rather than on what it costs. That puts pressure
on the part of the model that usually does the work. If the buyer cares about
ingredients, a mystery bag is not a bargain, it is a risk.

So the question for this project was whether the model survives that change, and what
would have to be different if it does.
